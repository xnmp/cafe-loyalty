Eastern Concourse Cafe and Bakery loyalty card

Collect a stamp each time you buy a drink. Collect 9 and your next drink is free.

Collecting a stamp

  • Android: open the app and hold the back of your phone against the back of the counter phone until both screens say the stamp was added.
  • iPhone: open the app, press Tap to collect, then hold the top of your iPhone against the back of the counter phone.
  • Not working, or no NFC? Press Scan code instead and scan the code on the counter phone's screen.

If a tap doesn't register, move the phones slowly. Every phone's NFC antenna is in a slightly different place, usually the middle of the back.

Using your free drink

When your card is full, a free drink ticket appears under it. When you order, open the ticket and hold your phone to the counter phone (on an iPhone, press Tap the counter phone first), or press Show my code for the barista to scan. Your free drink waits on your card until you use it.

New phone

Your card is stored only on your phone, with no account. It's included in your phone's normal backup (iCloud or Google), so restoring a backup onto a new phone usually brings your stamps with it. Uninstalling the app without a backup deletes the card.

Contact us

In the app, tap ⓘ and then Report a problem. It fills in the details we need, and you can attach a screenshot. Or email eastern-concourse-cafe-support@googlegroups.com.

Or just ask us at the counter.
