Privacy Policy

Eastern Concourse Cafe and Bakery loyalty card · Last updated 18 October 2026

The app collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

  • Your loyalty card: the stamps you have collected (each with the café's digital signature) and the rewards you have used, with their dates and times.
  • On the café's own counter phone only: a log of stamps handed out and a record of which stamps have been used for rewards, so the same stamp can't be used twice. It contains no names, phone numbers or other details about customers.

This data is stored only in the app's private storage on the device. It is included in your phone's normal backups (Google or iCloud), which are controlled by your phone's settings, not by us.

NFC and camera

When you tap your phone on the café's counter phone, the two phones exchange a stamp code over NFC, directly and only between those two devices. When you use a reward, your phone shows the counter phone the stamps it is using so they can be checked. The camera is used only to scan the stamp code on the counter screen; images are not saved or sent anywhere.

Links

The About screen can open the café's address in your maps app, its website or its Instagram page, but only when you tap them. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes your card from the phone. Your phone's backups may keep a copy until they are replaced or deleted in your phone's settings.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
